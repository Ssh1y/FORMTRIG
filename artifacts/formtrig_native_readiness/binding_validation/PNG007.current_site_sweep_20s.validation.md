## Benefit Evidence First

This is not positive benefit evidence for FORMTRIG.

All four repaired current-site BindingSpecs compile, pass lift/static mapping,
and reach the Magma `_T` oracle within the short sweep. However, the benefit
signal FORMTRIG needs is absent:

- `pretrigger_lift_guidance_ready=false` for all candidates.
- `saved_non_trigger_progress_events=0` for all candidates.
- `non_trigger_candidate_lift_delta=false`.
- `lift_delta_only_on_triggered_candidates=true`.

The terminal `_T` counts therefore do not show that FORMTRIG guided the
R-to-T process. They show that deleting or bypassing PLTE producer state can
reach the PNG007 terminal state, but the lifted signal only becomes useful once
the execution has already triggered.

## Mechanism Evidence

The native-site repair itself is valid. The 2026-06-16 native site map uses
Magma-prefixed symbols and different instruction numbers from the older B2
candidate:

- guard: `MAGMA_png_read_transform_info`, `pngrtran.c:1947`, cmp inst `19`
- root/use candidates: `MAGMA_png_read_transform_info`, `pngrtran.c:1967`,
  cmp/branch pairs `46/47`, `49/50`, `58/59`, and `70/71`
- opposite producer/input influence: `MAGMA_png_set_PLTE`, `pngset.c:634`,
  branch inst `81`

## Boundary

PNG007 should stay as control or negative evidence until a BindingSpec variant
produces accepted non-trigger progress and then beats faithful AFL++ family
baselines on same-budget terminal TTE. It should not consume main long-run
budget as a positive FORMTRIG case in its current form.
